## 1. Executive Summary

This content audit systematically reconciles institutional data, faculty records, degree descriptions, and curriculum checklists between the legacy/reference CPU CCS web presence, earlier project prototypes, and the official printed Academic Year 2024–2025 checklists. Every correction has been made using authoritative sources without inventing synthetic academic records. All checklist documents contain official typed endorsement blocks rather than digital/scanned signatures.

## 3. Verification Protocol & Quality Controls

1. **Zero Hallucination Standard:** No course code, unit value, lecture/lab hour breakdown, or faculty degree was invented. Every item is cross-referenced with the 4 official PDF checklists (with typed endorsement lines) and `https://ccs.cpu.edu.ph/`.
2. **Transparent Annotations:** Where historical or administrative transitions exist (e.g., OIC-Department Head vs. current Department Head; BSDMIA title punctuation), explicit notes are rendered in the UI and documented in the codebase.
3. **Responsive OBE Matrix:** The curriculum matrix supports multi-track switching, semester drilldowns, prerequisite checks, and full 4-year modal exploration without truncating academic data.
